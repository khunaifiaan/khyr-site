# KHYR brand

Read `references/DESIGN.md` first. It is the source of truth for color, type, shape, motion and voice.

## What KHYR is
- Small apps for the screen and for runs, by one independent maker. Market: worldwide, so English first (Indonesian as second language). Do not show the city on the website.
- All products are free for now (Gumroad $0+, pay what you want); income comes from tips via Saweria, Ko-fi and Trakteer.
- Products (catalog numbers): No.001 paandaa (dynamic island for Windows), No.002 Fieldnote (free Garmin Connect IQ watch face for Forerunner 55/165/255/265), No.003 Desk Garden (living zen-garden wallpaper, early access), No.004 Race Ready / Half Marathon Training Plan (Gumroad).
- Links: store khyrlabs.gumroad.com, Threads @khyr.labs, website khyr.my.id.

## Threads posts
- Style "Lab Card" with frame (see DESIGN.md section 6). 1080x1350 PNG.
- Formats: Tool find = 3-4 slides (cover, real screenshot, steps, tips). Run smart = 2-3 slides (claim, what research says, checklist) with source in reply 2/2. Build log = screen recording or before/after. Ask = text only or one image.
- Captions: hook line, 2-3 plain sentences, one personal take, a question. No link in the main text; source or link in the reply.
- Slide dots must match the real number of slides.
- Before posting a question, check it has not been asked before on the account.
- Facts must be checked; research claims must match what the study actually found.

## Website (khyr.my.id)
- Astro, bilingual (/en, /id), light and dark mode, CMS for products, work and notes, hosted on Cloudflare Pages.
- Sections: Tools (catalog), Work (gallery -> case study pages with "View live site"), Notes (from Threads), Support (Ko-fi for EN, Trakteer and Saweria with QR for ID).
- The island nav is the signature element; it shows the KHYR mark, mini Flo and the local time (no city name).
- Mascot Flo sits above the hero headline (see DESIGN.md section 7a).
- Page transitions: old view lifts and shrinks, new view rises behind a curtain (DESIGN.md section 7).

## Never
Watercolor, invented testimonials or numbers, "[K]" text instead of the logo, more than one highlighter per headline.
